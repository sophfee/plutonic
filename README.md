# Welcome to Plutonic
Plutonic is an open-source weapon base originally designed exclusively for use with Landis Games services. Using the outdated and lacking Longsword weapon base as reference, Plutonic was rebuilt, and optimized to the dot for maximum performance with some of the best effects and visuals out there to date.

## Information
- The content pack needed to use this weapon base can be found on the [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3469217810)
- If you are looking to develop with plutonic, you can use the [examples](https://github.com/sophfee/plutonic/tree/main/examples) as reference.
- Documentation is a work in progress!

## Console Commands
<dl>
  <dt><code>plutonic_centered</code></dt>  
  <dd>Centers the viewmodel, DOOM style.</dd>  
  
  <dt><code>plutonic_lowquality</code></dt>
  <dd>Performance mode, disables dynamic lighting from muzzleflashes and other effects that may be potentially taxing.</dd>
  
  <dt><code>plutonic_no_blur</code></dt>
  <dd>Disables ironsight blur.</dd>
  
  <dt><code>plutonic_debug</code></dt>
  <dd>Displays stats and other information on your active weapon.</dd>

  <dt><code>plutonic_debug_vm_attachments</code></dt>
  <dd>Prints attachment points on your active weapon.</dd>

  <dt><code>plutonic_debug_vm_bones</code></dt>
  <dd>Prints the bones for your active weapon.</dd>

  <dt><code>plutonic_debug_vm_sequences</code></dt>
  <dd>Prints the sequences for your active weapon, lengths, and numbers.</dd>

  <dt><code>plutonic_client_debug_attachments</code></dt>
  <dd>Prints attachments for your active weapon, equipped, non equipped and ent cache.</dd>
</dl>
